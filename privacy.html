Who we are

Shiryu Kit is a collection of free browser-based tools operated by Piyush Gahlawat ("we", "us"). For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we are the data controller for any personal data processed in connection with this website. For the purposes of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) ("DPDP Act"), we are the Data Fiduciary.

Contact for privacy matters: shiryukit.privacy@gmail.com

Please include "Privacy" or "Grievance" in the subject line so your request is routed correctly. We respond to all privacy requests within 30 days, and to grievances within the period stated in the Grievance Redressal section below.

What we process, and why

1. IP addresses and HTTP request metadata

Every website receives the IP address of the device connecting to it. This is technically necessary to serve a page over the internet. Our hosting and content delivery provider (Cloudflare) processes the IP address, browser user-agent, and requested URL as part of delivering the site and protecting it from abuse.

For operational debugging and security purposes, Cloudflare automatically retains request logs (including IP address and requested path) for a maximum of 3 days on our free-tier plan. We do not use these logs for profiling or analytics. We do not build user profiles. We do not sell or share this data.

GDPR legal basis: legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) — operating a functional, secure website.

Legal basis under the DPDP Act, 2023

The DPDP Act does not recognise a "legitimate interests" basis equivalent to GDPR Article 6(1)(f). It permits processing either with consent (Section 6) or under one of the specific legitimate uses listed in Section 7.

This site does not seek consent and does not operate user accounts. The only personal data we process is the IP address and request metadata that your browser necessarily sends when you load a page. We rely on the legitimate use in Section 7(a) — processing for the specified purpose for which you have voluntarily provided your personal data — on the basis that visiting the site is a voluntary act and the processing is limited to what is technically necessary to serve the page.

We recognise that the DPDP Act's legitimate-use list is closed and that this position may be clarified as the Data Protection Board of India issues guidance. We have documented the reasoning in our Records of Processing Activities, and we will update this notice if the position changes. We do not process special-category data, do not profile, do not sell data, and do not retain IP addresses beyond the transient edge request.

2. Files and text you enter into our tools

None of your files or text leaves your device. The QR code generator, password generator, word counter, JSON formatter, ATS resume cleaner, and resume templates all run entirely inside your browser using JavaScript. When you drop a DOCX file into the resume cleaner, it is processed in your browser's memory and never transmitted to any server.

You can verify this yourself: open your browser's developer tools, go to the Network tab, and use any tool. You will see no outgoing requests containing your data.

3. Cookies and tracking

We set no cookies. We do not use Google Analytics, advertising trackers, fingerprinting scripts, or any third-party marketing tools. There is no cookie banner on this site because there is nothing to consent to.

Cloudflare, our hosting provider, may set strictly necessary security cookies (such as __cf_bm and cf_clearance) to protect the site from automated abuse. These cookies are exempt from consent under the ePrivacy Directive because they are essential to the security of the service. They are not used for tracking, profiling, or advertising.

4. Third-party content delivery and processing

We load open-source JavaScript libraries from public CDNs (jsDelivr and cdnjs). These CDNs deliver static files to your browser and, like any web server, see the IP address making the request. They do not receive any of your data — only the request for the library file.

If you email our privacy contact address, your email and its metadata are processed by Google (Gmail).

Sub-processors:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — hosting, content delivery, and edge security (United States)
  • jsDelivr — serves open-source JavaScript libraries
  • cdnjs.cloudflare.com (Cloudflare) — serves open-source JavaScript libraries
  • Google LLC (Gmail) — receives and stores emails sent to our privacy contact address. Google's Data Processing Terms and standard contractual clauses cover international transfers.

How long we keep data

We do not retain personal data. Any transient request data processed by Cloudflare is governed by Cloudflare's own retention policies and data processing agreement with us. Emails sent to our privacy contact address are retained only as long as necessary to handle your request.

Your rights under GDPR and the DPDP Act

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the rights granted by the GDPR. If you are located in India, you have the rights granted by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"). Where the two overlap, we honour the stronger protection.

Your rights

  • Access — request a summary of the personal data we hold about you, and the identities of any third parties it has been shared with.
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate or misleading personal data.
  • Erasure — request deletion of personal data, subject to any legal retention requirement that applies to us.
  • Grievance redressal — raise a complaint about how we have handled your personal data, and receive a response within the period stated below.
  • Nomination (DPDP Act, Section 14) — nominate one or more individuals to exercise your rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity.
  • Restriction and objection (GDPR) — request that we restrict processing, or object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Complaint to a supervisory authority — you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority, or with the Data Protection Board of India.

How to exercise a right, or make a nomination

Email the Grievance Officer at the address above. Include enough information to identify you — for a DPDP request this is the email address you are contacting us from, and any identifier you have used with us. We do not require an account or a customer number because we do not operate user accounts.

Because we do not store personal data beyond transient IP addresses that are not retained, the response to most requests will be a statement of what we do not hold. We will still respond within the timelines above, and we will confirm in writing what processing, if any, applies to you.

Identity verification

We verify requests by reply-to-address matching the original request. Since no persistent personal data is held, the substantive response is the same regardless of identity, and there is no risk of disclosure to an impersonator.

Data Protection Officer / Significant Data Fiduciary

We are not classified as a Significant Data Fiduciary under Section 10 of the DPDP Act, and we are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer. The Grievance Officer is the correct contact for all data protection matters.

International transfers

Our infrastructure provider Cloudflare operates a global network. Requests from EU users may be processed at Cloudflare data centres outside the EU. Cloudflare's Data Processing Addendum and Standard Contractual Clauses provide appropriate safeguards for these transfers. Emails sent to our privacy contact address are processed by Google, whose standard contractual clauses provide equivalent safeguards.

Children's privacy

These tools are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we materially change this policy, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

No EU representative required

Under Article 27 of the GDPR, a representative in the EU is only required for controllers outside the EU that process personal data on a large scale or as a core activity. Our processing is limited to transient IP addresses incidental to hosting static files, which does not meet that threshold.
