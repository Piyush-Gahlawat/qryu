Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Who we are

Shiryu Kit is a collection of free browser-based tools operated by Piyush Gahlawat ("we", "us"). For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we are the data controller for any personal data processed in connection with this website.

Contact for privacy matters: shiryukit.privacy@gmail.com

Please include "Privacy" in the subject line so your request is routed correctly. We respond to all privacy requests within 30 days.

What we process, and why

1. IP addresses and HTTP request metadata

Every website receives the IP address of the device connecting to it. This is technically necessary to serve a page over the internet. Our hosting and content delivery provider (Cloudflare) processes the IP address, browser user-agent, and requested URL as part of delivering the site and protecting it from abuse.

We do not actively log, store, correlate, or analyse individual IP addresses. We do not build user profiles. We do not sell or share this data.

Legal basis: legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) — operating a functional, secure website.

2. Files and text you enter into our tools

None of your files or text leaves your device. The QR code generator, password generator, word counter, JSON formatter, ATS resume cleaner, and resume templates all run entirely inside your browser using JavaScript. When you drop a DOCX file into the resume cleaner, it is processed in your browser's memory and never transmitted to any server.

You can verify this yourself: open your browser's developer tools, go to the Network tab, and use any tool. You will see no outgoing requests containing your data.

3. Cookies and tracking

We set no cookies. We do not use Google Analytics, advertising trackers, fingerprinting scripts, or any third-party marketing tools. There is no cookie banner on this site because there is nothing to consent to.

4. Third-party content delivery and processing

We load open-source JavaScript libraries from public CDNs (jsDelivr and cdnjs). These CDNs deliver static files to your browser and, like any web server, see the IP address making the request. They do not receive any of your data — only the request for the library file.

If you email our privacy contact address, your email and its metadata are processed by Google (Gmail).

Sub-processors:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — hosting and content delivery (United States)
  • jsDelivr — serves open-source JavaScript libraries
  • cdnjs.cloudflare.com (Cloudflare) — serves open-source JavaScript libraries
  • Google LLC (Gmail) — receives and stores emails sent to our privacy contact address. Google's Data Processing Terms and standard contractual clauses cover international transfers.

How long we keep data

We do not retain personal data. Any transient request data processed by Cloudflare is governed by Cloudflare's own retention policies and data processing agreement with us. Emails sent to our privacy contact address are retained only as long as necessary to handle your request.

Your rights under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the right to:

  • Access any personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

Because we do not store any personal data, in practice the only data we could disclose is the transient IP address that Cloudflare processes when you visit. You can email us at the address above for any request, and we will respond within 30 days.

International transfers

Our infrastructure provider Cloudflare operates a global network. Requests from EU users may be processed at Cloudflare data centres outside the EU. Cloudflare's Data Processing Addendum and Standard Contractual Clauses provide appropriate safeguards for these transfers. Emails sent to our privacy contact address are processed by Google, whose standard contractual clauses provide equivalent safeguards.

Children's privacy

These tools are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we materially change this policy, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

No EU representative required

Under Article 27 of the GDPR, a representative in the EU is only required for controllers outside the EU that process personal data on a large scale or as a core activity. Our processing is limited to transient IP addresses incidental to hosting static files, which does not meet that threshold.
